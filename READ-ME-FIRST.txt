Mktforge — Links into a module land at its top
==============================================

UPLOAD ONLY  assets/js/app.js
             (open the assets/js folder first, then upload)

No Worker deploy. After uploading, hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) before checking.

WHAT CHANGED

  Nav-bar buttons — unchanged: they take you back to where you left off in
  that module. Clicking the button for the module you're already on leaves
  you where you are.

  Everything else now lands at the TOP of the module:
    - the Mktforge logo (also when you're already on My Company)
    - Manage Profile in the account menu
    - links inside a module (e.g. Draft Messaging's "go to Build Positioning")
    - a typed or shared URL, and the browser's back/forward buttons

  Before, all of these put you back wherever you'd last scrolled to.
