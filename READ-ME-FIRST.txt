WHAT CHANGED

  Data now lives in users/{uid}/companies/{companyId}/... instead of directly
  on the account. The account keeps only account things: email, profile
  picture, the company to open next time, and the lists behind the 10-company
  limit and the unique-name check.

  The rest of the app keeps working unchanged on top of this, with one company
  per account. The first time a tab touches data, it picks its company: the
  one it was on (refresh), else the account's last company, else the oldest.
  A brand-new account gets its first company automatically.

  Built in for later sessions: add / switch / delete company, the unique-name
  and 10-company checks (both in transactions, so two tabs can't beat them),
  last-company tracking, and a "company switched" signal that is separate
  from a profile change. That separation is the Market Tracker fix: switching
  can no longer look like competitors being removed.

  The rules refuse any write into a company that's being deleted, and make
  the old pre-company data read-only, so nothing can change it between the
  copy and the cleanup.

TESTED HERE

  - The data layer, in the real app (preview mode), 21 checks: old data became
    the first company, switching, handles keeping their company after a
    switch, duplicate names, ordering, the 10 limit, delete, refresh, two tabs,
    sign-out then sign-in, and My Company still rendering.
  - The migration script, 40 checks, including: a repeat copy after go-live
    never overwrites or deletes work done in the new app; a half-finished
    cleanup can't be undone by copy; a company deleted in the app isn't
    brought back.
  - An independent review of all three files; every finding is fixed.
