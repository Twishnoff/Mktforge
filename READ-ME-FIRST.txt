Mktforge — Market Tracker: crawl coverage + ledger cleanup (site files)
=======================================================================

Supersedes the previous zip. Both a site upload AND a Worker deploy.

1. COPY over the repo, keeping the paths:

       modules/market-tracker/market-tracker.js
       README.md

   If you never uploaded the zip before this one, also take its
   market-tracker.css and assets/js/data.js — this zip doesn't carry them.

2. DEPLOY THE WORKER:

       cd C:\Users\Tyler\customer-tracker
       npx wrangler deploy

3. WHAT CHANGED

   a) CRAWL COVERAGE. A recent blog post could be found on the site and
      never opened, so it never got a date and never appeared. Two causes:

      - The section index pages to expand were taken in the order the links
        appeared on the homepage, capped at 8. A site with a long nav
        (products, solutions, use cases, customers, platform, features,
        news, blog...) could push /blog past the cap, so the blog index was
        never expanded at all.
      - Pages were then opened in discovery order against a 40-page budget.
        Homepage links come first, so dozens of product and solution pages
        were opened before any blog post was reached.

      Now: one index per section is picked before the cap is filled, pages
      are opened section by section in turn (blog, news, stories, product),
      the budget is 80 pages read five at a time, and anything the budget
      cuts off is COUNTED and reported, so this can't fail silently again.

   b) LEDGER CLEANUP (from the previous zip, if you haven't applied it):
      deleting a competitor from My Company now clears its history whether
      or not a box still exists.

4. AFTER DEPLOYING, hit Refresh Data on the ClickHouse box. The run will be
   slower than before — it is opening a lot more pages.

   The blog posts it finds this time will be new to the ledger, so they
   should appear. If a recent post still doesn't, look at the line under
   the box: "N found but not opened" means the page limit is still the
   constraint, and "N whose publish date couldn't be confirmed" means the
   post was opened but showed no date the agent could read.

5. No Firestore change.
