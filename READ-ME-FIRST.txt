WHAT'S NEW

  Switching companies no longer reloads the page. Results on screen stay with
  their company: switch away and back and they're still there (until you
  refresh or close the tab; saved data is always kept).

  Build Positioning, Draft Messaging and Market Tracker now work like the
  other modules:
    - their own screen per company; typed inputs and unsaved answers are
      kept and survive a refresh
    - switching mid-run stops the run (the Worker request is really
      cancelled) with "Run stopped when you switched companies. Run it
      again." A Draft Answer row says "Draft stopped…"; a Market Tracker box
      says "Run stopped… Refresh Data to run it again."
    - a refresh mid-run shows the red light and "This run was interrupted."
  Market Tracker's competitor and title history is kept per company, so
  editing one company's My Company can never clear another's.

  Also fixed while testing:
    - saved materials are read from the company a run started on
    - a PDF is saved to the company it was made for, never another one
    - clicking a module while a switch is in progress opens it afterwards
