Mktforge — Target Messaging: By Job Title drafting agent
========================================================

By Job Title now runs the real drafting agent. By Individual is unchanged
(still the Hello World placeholder).

1) target-messaging Worker
   Copy worker/src/index.js and worker/src/agent.js into the Worker's src
   folder (the old index.js is kept as src/index.js.bak-2026-09-25 in
   Downloads\target-messaging-worker_1\target-messaging, where they've
   already been put). Then, from that Worker folder:

       npx wrangler secret put ANTHROPIC_API_KEY     (paste the same key your other Workers use)
       npx wrangler deploy

   Optional vars: ANTHROPIC_MODEL (default claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929),
   FAST_MODEL (default claude-haiku-4-5-20251001).

2) Site file (repo, same path)
       modules/target-messaging/target-messaging.js
   Hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) after uploading.

WHAT A BY JOB TITLE RUN DOES
   - The page now also sends every Persona Builder and Battle Card PDF on the
     company (newest 15 of each) along with the sources you chose.
   - A quick pass reads the titles on each; personas whose Primary or
     Secondary Job Title match qualify, and their secondary titles decide
     which battle cards (by Target Job Title) qualify.
   - Research pass: up to 10 challenges ranked by frequency, then re-ranked
     by how well the company's documented offerings solve them. Battle card
     boxes > persona Immediate Work Priorities > Development Priorities;
     points in both a battle card and a persona are marked most important;
     your chosen sources are treated as the newest and most accurate.
     Nothing invented: every solution needs a quote from your files/site.
   - Writing pass: top 3 (or fewer) → cold email ("Hi [FIRST_NAME],") and
     landing page copy, in the voice of your imported files, Build
     Positioning / Draft Messaging files and website, guided by any
     testimonial from that title.
   - Website box ticked: homepage plus up to 7 product / customer / case
     study pages are read.
   - A run takes roughly 1–3 minutes.
