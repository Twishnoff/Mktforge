Mktforge — Multi-company, Session 2: company dropdown and switching
===================================================================

No rules change, no Worker deploy. Hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) after uploading.

WHAT'S NEW

  Company dropdown in the management bar, left of your account menu, with a
  thin grey line between them.
    - Shows the active company's name; long names cut off at 26 characters
      with "...", full name on hover.
    - Lists your companies: named ones A-Z, then unnamed "Company X" ones.
    - Bottom row: green + Add New Company. It makes a new empty company,
      switches to it and opens My Company. At 10 companies the row turns red:
      "10 Company Limit Reached".
    - Updates as soon as you save a new Company Name — in any open tab.

  Switching (or adding) while a module is running asks first:
    "Switching companies will stop Find My Customer. Switch anyway?"
  Cancel changes nothing. Nothing else on the page works while it's open.

  My Company: saving a name another of your companies uses is blocked with
  "A company with this name already exists in your account. Choose a
  different name." (ignores capitals and extra spaces).

  If another tab deletes the company this tab is on, this tab moves to your
  oldest company and says why.

FOR NOW (until Sessions 3-4 update each module)

  Switching reloads the page into the other company. That stops any run
  cleanly, but results on screen and unsaved typing are cleared by a switch
  rather than kept for when you come back. Saved data (My Company, files,
  Build Positioning answers, Market Tracker) is always kept per company.

  There's no Delete This Company button yet (Session 3), so a company you add
  now stays until then.
