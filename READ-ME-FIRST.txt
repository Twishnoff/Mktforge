Mktforge — Market Tracker: ledger cleanup + stats wording (site files)
======================================================================

Site files only. The Worker is unchanged since the last deploy — if you have
already run `npx wrangler deploy` since the off-site date fix, you do NOT
need to deploy again.

1. COPY over the repo, keeping the paths:

       modules/market-tracker/market-tracker.js
       README.md

   This supersedes the market-tracker.js in the previous zip. If you never
   uploaded that one, upload its market-tracker.css, assets/js/data.js and
   README.md too — this zip doesn't carry them.

2. WHAT CHANGED

   a) The ledger now follows My Company's competitor list instead of the
      boxes. Deleting a competitor's URL from My Company clears its history
      whether or not a box still exists at that moment.

      The bug: Stop Tracking deliberately KEEPS the ledger. The only code
      that deleted a ledger lived inside "remove this box". So if you pressed
      Stop Tracking first and removed the competitor from My Company second,
      nothing was left to trigger the deletion, and re-adding that competitor
      picked up its old history.

   b) The stats line under a competitor box no longer collapses four
      different reasons into "already shown to you before".

3. CLEARING THE CLICKHOUSE LEDGER YOU ALREADY HAVE

   The fix is not retroactive — that stale ledger is still in Firestore.
   After uploading this file, with the app open:

       1. Remove clickhouse.com from Competitors in My Company and save.
          (This now deletes the ledger.)
       2. Re-add clickhouse.com and save.
       3. Track Competitor again — it will run as a true first run.

   Same steps for any other competitor you removed and re-added earlier.

4. No Firestore change.
