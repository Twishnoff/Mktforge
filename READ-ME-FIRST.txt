WHAT'S NEW

  Delete This Company (My Company, right of the title; only when you have
  more than one company). The warning modal from the spec, with
  "No, Don't Delete" and a red "Yes, Delete". Yes stops anything running,
  deletes the company and moves you to your oldest one. If the tab closes
  part-way, the clean-up finishes at your next sign-in.

  Typed text is kept per company and survives a refresh (Persona Builder,
  Battle Card Generator, Find My Customer, Marketing Opportunities, and
  unsaved edits on My Company). Company URL fills in from THAT company's
  My Company.

  Runs:
    - Switching company mid-run stops it; back on that company the module
      shows how it looked before the run, with "Run stopped when you
      switched companies. Run it again." (goes away once you type or run)
    - A refresh, closed tab or sign-out mid-run: on return, a red light and
      "This run was interrupted. Run it again." Red clears on click/scroll.
    - Files you import finish into the company you dropped them into.
