WHAT'S NEW

  My Company has an "Integrations" section between the profile and Your
  Saved Resources, with a HubSpot card for the active company:
    - Not connected: "Connect HubSpot" sends you to HubSpot's approval
      screen, then back to My Company with a notice.
    - Connected: "Connected to <portal> · read-only access" + Disconnect
      (asks to confirm).
    - Needs reconnecting (token revoked/expired): Reconnect + Disconnect.
  Each Mktforge company has its own HubSpot connection.

  config.js: new hubspot.API_BASE_URL.
  module-kit.js: MktforgeKit.hubspot() — the shared client later modules
  will use for HubSpot data (status / connect / disconnect / call).
