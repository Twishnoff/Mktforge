Mktforge — Market Tracker: new job-title agent rules
====================================================

Site upload AND a Worker deploy.

1. COPY over the repo, keeping the paths:

       assets/js/extract.js                            reads PDF link annotations
       assets/js/data.js                               title ledgers, links-aware text
       modules/market-tracker/market-tracker.js        report parsing, ledger, copy
       modules/marketing-opportunities/opportunities-pdf.js   prints URLs
       modules/persona-builder/persona-pdf.js                 prints URLs

2. DEPLOY THE WORKER:

       cd C:\Users\Tyler\customer-tracker
       npx wrangler deploy

3. WHAT CHANGED — job-title boxes

   SOURCES  Reddit, Hacker News, Medium, Substack, G2, Capterra, TrustRadius,
            plus every site named in the Where They Gather section of a
            persona for this title and in the Publications, Other Syndication
            Platforms, Influencers and Social Media and Blogs lists of a
            Marketing Opportunities report for this title or a secondary one.
            A persona naming r/dataengineering is passed through as that
            subreddit, not flattened to "reddit.com".

   REVIEWS  A G2/Capterra/TrustRadius review is only surfaced when the
            reviewer's own listed job title is the tracked title or one of
            its secondaries. Never inferred.

   WINDOW   30 days, down from 90.

   LEDGER   Each title now has one. A refresh reports what you haven't been
            shown, with a 24-hour carry-over. Kept by Stop Tracking, deleted
            when the title leaves My Company. An empty box is now a normal
            result and says "No new activity found since your last refresh."

   COLOUR   Only about your company. Positive green, negative red, everything
            else uncoloured. Competitor complaints are no longer green.

   VOICE    First person is required for challenges content only. A neutral
            article naming your company or a competitor now qualifies.

   BLOCKED  Your own and competitors' domains, content authored by them
            anywhere (a competitor's Medium post is still the competitor),
            and vendor blogs about their own market.

4. WHAT CHANGED — PDF links (this is why extract.js and the two PDF files
   are in here)

   The Marketing Opportunities report printed "Visit Site" and attached the
   address as a click target; Persona Builder printed "Name (type)" the same
   way. Text extraction never saw either, so the site lists the new rules
   depend on were invisible. Extraction now reads link annotations, and both
   generators print the address as text.

   Reports already in your account are re-read automatically the first time
   Market Tracker needs one. That run is slower.

5. No Firestore change. Title ledgers live in the existing competitorLedger
   collection under t- ids, so the published rules already cover them.
