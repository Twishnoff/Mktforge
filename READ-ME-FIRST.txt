Mktforge — Market Tracker: posts count as challenges content
=============================================================

Follow-up to the job-title zip. If you haven't uploaded that one yet, do it
first — it carried extract.js, data.js and the two PDF generators, which this
zip does not.

1. COPY over the repo:

       modules/market-tracker/market-tracker.js      (one stats label)

2. DEPLOY THE WORKER — this is where the actual change is:

       cd C:\Users\Tyler\customer-tracker
       npx wrangler deploy

3. WHAT CHANGED

   The challenges category used to accept only comments and first-person
   blog articles, so a top-level Reddit post about a listed priority was
   never collected. Forum and social posts now count, and neither posts nor
   comments have to be written in the first person.

   Blog articles still do: an article only counts as challenges content when
   the writer is recounting something they did, not explaining a topic.

   Competitor and company mentions were never restricted this way and are
   unchanged.

4. EXPECT MORE ROWS, AND MORE NOISE. Dropping the voice requirement for
   posts means general discussion threads can now qualify on subject alone.
   If the boxes get noisy, the knob to turn back is requiring first person
   for posts while leaving comments open.

5. No Firestore change.
