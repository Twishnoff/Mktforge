Mktforge — Market Tracker: relative dates + a box that explains itself
======================================================================

1. UPLOAD:  modules/market-tracker/market-tracker.js

2. DEPLOY:  cd C:\Users\Tyler\customer-tracker
            npx wrangler deploy

3. WHAT CHANGED

   a) RELATIVE TIMESTAMPS COUNT AS DATES. The agent was told to open the
      page, read the publish date, and drop anything it couldn't confirm —
      never guess. Reddit and Hacker News don't print dates. They print
      "7d ago". Under that instruction the agent had to throw those away
      itself, before anything reached the filters, which is why the box said
      "0 items found" with nothing listed as dropped.

      It now works the real date out from today's date, marks it approximate,
      and the row shows it with a ~. An item is only left out when the page
      gives no indication at all of when it was posted.

   b) THE BOX NOW SAYS WHAT STEERED IT. Under the results:

         "Guided by Persona — Data Engineer.pdf, Marketing
          Opportunities.pdf, and 6 sites they name."

      or, when nothing matched:

         "No persona, opportunity list or customer report matched this
          title, so the agent searched only the default sources and could
          only count posts where the author states their job title. Run
          Persona Builder for this title to change that."

      That second message is the one to watch for. It means the reports
      aren't being read, which on its own guarantees an empty box — with no
      priorities to match against, the agent falls back to needing the
      author to state their job title outright, and on a forum nobody does.

4. No Firestore change.
