Two pieces, in this order.

1) customer-tracker Worker  (C:\Users\Tyler\customer-tracker)
   Already updated in place on your computer. The files it replaced are kept
   next to them as *.bak-2026-09-24. New file: src/channels.js. Deploy it:

       cd C:\Users\Tyler\customer-tracker
       npx wrangler deploy

   No new secrets, no new bindings. Check afterwards:
   https://customer-tracker.tyler-wishnoff.workers.dev/api/track should

   (job title 8, competitor 10) because tracked channels are read first.
   Reddit channels are read directly only if the Reddit secrets are set;
   otherwise they're searched instead.

WHAT CHANGED
   My Company         new "Tracked News and Media URLs" tag field under
                      Competitors. Type a URL, Enter to add, x to remove.
   Marketing Opps     Track Channel / Stop Tracking on rows in Influencers,
                      Publications, Other Syndication Platforms, Social Media
                      and Blogs. Saves the channel, not the single page:
                      Medium post -> author, YouTube video -> channel,
                      Reddit thread -> subreddit, blog post -> blog index,
                      news story -> publication.
   Market Tracker     "Additional Tracked Channels:" line (read-only tags +
                      reminder note). Every refresh reads each channel in
                      depth and labels rows found there "Tracked channel".
