WHAT'S NEW

  from the hubspot-connect Worker. It's best effort: it never blocks or
  slows the delete, and the request is sent so it survives the page move.
  (Mktforge stays installed in the HubSpot account itself until someone
  uninstalls it under Connected Apps there; with the token gone, Mktforge
  can no longer read anything.)
