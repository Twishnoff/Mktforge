Mktforge — Market Tracker: Discourse forum detection
====================================================

Replaces the Reddit zip (whose Reddit-app steps no longer apply).

1. UPLOAD  modules/market-tracker/market-tracker.js
           Open the modules/market-tracker folder on github.com FIRST,
           then Add file -> Upload files.

2. DEPLOY  cd C:\Users\Tyler\customer-tracker
           npx wrangler deploy
           (src/index.js and src/sources.js are already on your machine.
            No secrets needed.)

3. WHAT IT DOES

   Detection only — nothing is read from the forums yet. On each job-title
   run the Worker checks the sites your reports name and asks two things:
   is this a Discourse forum, and does its robots.txt let a tool read its
   topic listings?

   The answer appears at the end of the line under the box, e.g.

     "Of the 15 sites your reports name, 2 are Discourse forums
      (dbt Community, Airbyte Forum) — not read yet."

   or "None of the 15 sites your reports name is a Discourse forum."

   Reddit, Hacker News, Medium, Substack, YouTube and GitHub are skipped —
   none of them is Discourse. Each site gets a few seconds; a slow one is
   given up on rather than holding up the run.

   we stop here.
